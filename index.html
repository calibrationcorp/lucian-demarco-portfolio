The person behind the work

A mind for systems.
A drive to create.

Michael L. DeMarco is a designer, permit expediter and construction document technologist whose work connects design thinking with the realities of building, coordination and delivery. Across more than two decades, he has developed a broad, hands-on perspective shaped by projects, collaboration, problem-solving and continual learning.

He is also a digital creator—developing platforms and tools that bring complex information into clearer, more useful forms. The common thread is a belief that good ideas should become things people can use.

The long view

A path measured in persistence.

The journey toward becoming a licensed architect has not been a straight line. It has included difficult chapters, setbacks, pauses and the work of finding a way forward. Those experiences are part of the journey, but they are not the measure of a person’s vision, ability or future.

What matters here is the commitment that continues: learning, building, refining ideas, taking responsibility for the work and pursuing the next milestone. The goal of licensure remains alive—not as a claim of status, but as a direction that continues to shape the work ahead.

“I am defined by what I build, what I learn, and the determination to keep moving forward.”

Every project, platform and new idea is another expression of that drive. This portfolio is a record of work in progress—and a statement that the vision is still moving.

01 · Initial consultation and project brief ＋

Client meeting

Discuss the property, intended use, goals, budget, schedule and priorities. Review any existing plans, photos or records and identify the decisions needed to get started.

Scope and proposal

Define deliverables, milestones, review rounds, consultant needs and responsibilities. Agree on the scope, fees and communication plan before design begins.

02 · Existing conditions and feasibility ＋

Site and building documentation

Review available surveys and drawings, document visible conditions and prepare base plans as scoped. Coordinate survey, testing or specialist investigations where needed.

Feasibility and due diligence

Review constraints such as setbacks, access, existing systems and intended occupancy. Compare options and flag budget, approval and sequencing questions for early decisions.

03 · Concept design and visualization ＋

Space planning and design options

Develop layouts, circulation, exterior concepts and phasing options. Review them with the client and refine the preferred direction against the brief.

Preconstruction visualization and sales materials

When included, prepare renderings, walkthroughs and presentation materials for client reviews, stakeholder discussions or presales.

04 · Design development and consultant coordination ＋

Materials, systems and scope refinement

Develop dimensions, assemblies, material selections and interfaces between trades. Coordinate budget and constructability feedback before the drawing set is finalized.

Consultant team coordination

Help identify and coordinate structural, mechanical, electrical, plumbing, fire protection, civil or acoustical specialists as the project calls for. Coordinate with the architect of record where applicable and confirm each consultant’s deliverables.

05 · Construction documents and permitting ＋

Permit and construction drawing sets

Prepare scoped plans, elevations, sections, details and schedules. Coordinate consultant drawings and CSI-formatted specifications where included, and route documents for the applicable professional review and sign-off.

Permit expediting

Confirm the submission checklist, assemble the application package, coordinate filings, track reviews and organize responses and revisions through permit issuance.

06 · Contractor selection and preconstruction ＋

Bid support and contractor selection

Issue the scoped bid documents, respond to bidder questions, compare proposals and review exclusions, allowances and lead times with the client.

Preconstruction handoff

Coordinate the kickoff with the owner, contractor and consultants. Review the current drawings, schedule, inspection milestones and process for questions and changes.

07 · Construction-phase support ＋

Questions, submittals and changes

Coordinate responses to requests for information, shop drawings and product submittals. Document revisions and help the client track proposed scope, schedule or cost changes.

Site observations and inspection coordination

Conduct site observations as scoped, record issues for follow-up and coordinate questions with the contractor and responsible consultants. Help track inspection milestones from foundation through final finishes.

08 · Final inspections, occupancy and closeout ＋

Punch list and final approvals

Walk the project with the team as scoped, document incomplete items and track corrections. Coordinate final inspections and certificate-of-occupancy paperwork where applicable.

Owner handoff and closeout records

Compile available contractor record drawings, operating manuals, warranties, inspection records and approvals. Review outstanding items and hand off the closeout package to the owner.
