The person behind the work

A mind for systems.
A drive to create.

Michael, L. De Marco is a designer, permit expediter and construction document technologist whose work connects design thinking with the realities of building, coordination and delivery. Across more than two decades, he has developed a broad, hands-on perspective shaped by projects, collaboration, problem-solving and continual learning.

He is also a digital creator—developing platforms and tools that bring complex information into clearer, more useful forms. The common thread is a belief that good ideas should become things people can use.

The long view

A path measured in persistence.

The journey toward becoming a licensed architect has not been a straight line. It has included difficult chapters, setbacks, pauses and the work of finding a way forward. Those experiences are part of the journey, but they are not the measure of a person’s vision, ability or future.

What matters here is the commitment that continues: learning, building, refining ideas, taking responsibility for the work and pursuing the next milestone. The goal of licensure remains alive—not as a claim of status, but as a direction that continues to shape the work ahead.

“I am defined by what I build, what I learn, and the determination to keep moving forward.”

Every project, platform and new idea is another expression of that drive. This portfolio is a record of work in progress—and a statement that the vision is still moving.

Technical documentation, thoughtfully coordinated.

Michael prepares and coordinates construction documentation as a Construction Document Technologist, including layouts, elevations, sections, envelope details, foundation and roof coordination, schedules and project specifications.

Drawing packages are organized for communication, constructibility, pricing and review, with CSI specifications conventions applied as appropriate to project scope.

Specialists aligned around one project.

Consultant coordination can include:

  • Structural, civil and geotechnical engineering
  • HVAC, mechanical, electrical and plumbing
  • Fire protection and life safety
  • Acoustical, energy and building-envelope design
  • Surveyors, contractors and specialty trades
Professional responsibility by jurisdiction. Where required, an appropriately licensed Architect of Record is engaged for the specific project and location and retains legally required responsible supervision, review, approval and sealing duties. Specialty engineers remain responsible for their professional designs. SimpleArchStudios provides documentation, design and coordination services within applicable licensing rules; Michael is not represented as licensed.
